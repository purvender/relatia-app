# Relatia Discovery — Evidence Register

> **Document Classification:** Internal Single Source of Truth for Customer Discovery Signals  
> **Status:** Active Working Register — Day 10 Step 4  
> **Methodology:** Tracks qualitative and quantitative evidence gathered across customer and provider discovery interviews.  
> **Validation Criteria:** An operational assumption is NOT considered validated by desk research or solitary interviews.  
> **Validation Thresholds:**
> - `Unvalidated`: 0–1 interviews with anecdotal or conflicting feedback.
> - `Weak Signal`: 2–4 interviews supporting the assumption without quantitative depth.
> - `Repeated Signal`: 5+ independent interviews describing the exact friction without prompting.
> - `Provisionally Validated`: 5+ interviews + at least 3 spontaneous mentions + at least 2 quantified data points + 1 defined pilot test path.
> - `Validated`: Proven through real pilot execution (e.g., successful 3-event validation pilot).
> - `Contradicted`: Significant empirical evidence demonstrating that the assumption is invalid or rejected by customers.
