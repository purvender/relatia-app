## 1. Purpose & Scope of Day 10 Step 4

The purpose of Day 10 Step 4 is to establish an unassailable, operational outreach and discovery system for Relatia.

Up to this point, all personas, spend values, event frequencies, and buying dynamics represent **structured working hypotheses**. We do not build features, negotiate long-term enterprise pricing, or deploy sales capital until these assumptions are tested directly against real practitioners in our target market.

This playbook provides the scripts, workflows, qualification criteria, and evidence-gathering rigor needed to book, conduct, and synthesize **20 focused, non-promotional discovery interviews** across enterprise decision-makers and venue operators in Gurugram and Delhi–NCR.

---

## 2. Core Research Principles & Ethics

To ensure interviews yield empirical behavioral evidence rather than polite, misleading compliments, all team members must follow five non-negotiable rules:

1. **Do NOT Lead with the Solution:** Never pitch Relatia, demo the software, or discuss our features during the first 15–20 minutes of the call.
2. **Anchor on the Last Real Event:** Never ask *"Would you like a tool that does X?"* or *"How much would you pay for Y?"* Instead ask: *"Tell me about the last executive dinner your team hosted. What exact steps occurred? What went wrong? How was it paid?"*
3. **Opinions are NOT Evidence:** A respondent saying *"That sounds like a great idea"* is worthless signal. Evidence consists of concrete past actions: hours spent, money wasted, receipts rejected, or workarounds actively employed.
4. **Zero Unverified Claims or Guarantees:** Do not promise guaranteed bookings, guaranteed 100% GST recovery, autonomous AI negotiation, or production ERP integrations.
5. **Strict Data Protection & Privacy:** Never record without prior verbal/written consent, and never commit personal contact data, customer corporate secrets, or real GSTINs to the Git repository.

### ICP Qualification Criteria
Before adding a contact to [`outreach-tracker-template.csv`](file:///Users/purvenderhooda/Documents/hooda/relatia-app/docs/customer-discovery/outreach-tracker-template.csv), verify that they satisfy at least three criteria:

- **Headcount:** 50 to 500 employees (autonomous departmental budget speed).
- **Event Cadence:** Regularly hosts client-facing dinners, executive prospect hospitality, partner meetings, or leadership celebrations (estimated ≥2 events/quarter).

- Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) or organizations requiring formal multi-month government tenders for any trial.
- Companies with strict global travel/entertainment freezes.
- Venues that operate exclusively as informal retail bars without B2B tax invoicing capability.

## 5. Follow-Up Sequences & Referral Protocol

Response rates increase significantly with a respectful, spaced follow-up cadence. We maintain a strict rule: **Maximum of two follow-ups. Never spam.**

```
[Day 0: Initial Outreach] 
         |
    (3 business days of no response)
         v
[Day 3: Gentle Follow-Up 1] 
         |
    (3 business days of no response)
         v
[Day 6: Courteous Close-Out Follow-Up 2] 
         |
    (No response -> Mark UNRESPONSIVE and STOP)
```

### Follow-Up 1 (Day 3 — Gentle Bump)
> *"Hi [First Name], following up briefly on my earlier note. Completely understand you are juggling priorities. If you have 20 minutes later this week to share your experience with corporate dining workflows, I’d be immensely grateful. If not, no worries at all! Best, Purvender"*

## 7. Evidence Scoring & Validation Thresholds

Every interview must be scored objectively based on observable empirical facts, not subjective impressions:

### Pain Intensity Scoring (1 to 5)
- **1 (Negligible):** Handles dining seamlessly; spends <30 mins; no invoice or finance complaints.
- **2 (Minor Friction):** Occasional delay booking a venue; finance occasionally asks for missing bills.
- **3 (Moderate Problem):** Loses 2–4 hours per event; frequent WhatsApp chasing; occasional tax credit write-offs.
- **4 (Acute Frustration):** Loses 5+ hours; frequent budget overspends; acute GST rejection friction with finance.
- **5 (Severe Bottleneck):** Experienced major client embarrassment, lost significant tax credits, or leadership mandated an immediate operational audit.

### Validation Gate Criteria
A persona or market assumption is **NOT validated** unless:
1. **Sample Size:** At least **5 independent interviews** support the assumption.
2. **Spontaneous Mention:** At least **3 respondents** bring up the exact problem without prompting.
3. **Quantified Evidence:** At least **2 respondents** provide concrete, quantified metrics (e.g., hours spent, rupees lost, days delayed).
4. **Concrete Pilot Path:** At least **1 respondent** defines an actionable path to test a 3-event validation pilot.
5. **Zero Unresolved Contradictions:** No major empirical counter-evidence remains unexplained.
