By unifying event creation, multi-tier approvals, venue contracting, payment escrow, and post-event tax reconciliation into a single deterministic state machine, Relatia helps enterprises recapture up to 18% in input tax credits while unlocking measurable relationship velocity across strategic client accounts.

* **Direct Bottom-Line Savings**: Corporate dining in India attracts 5% to 18% GST. When employees swipe cards at restaurants and submit fragmented receipts, over 65% of tax credits are lost due to missing company GSTINs or incorrect invoices. * **Proactive Budget Governance**: Enforce dynamic spend policies before money is spent. Set per-head thresholds by client tier (e.g. ₹4,000 for regular accounts; ₹10,000 for CXO tier) with automatic routing to department heads for exceptions.

## 10. Customer Problem Statement

Enterprises across India invest heavily in executive hospitality to cultivate key accounts and executive talent, yet manage the process using 20-year-old administrative tools:
* **Friction in Discovery**: Executive assistants waste dozens of hours calling venues, negotiating room minimums, and manually collecting dietary profiles.
* **Governance Blindspots**: Lack of pre-spend approval leads to unauthorized budget overruns and awkward post-facto expense rejections.
* **Tax Inefficiency**: Misplaced restaurant receipts and non-compliant GST bills cause enterprises to forfeit millions in legitimate input tax credits.
* **Disconnected Operations**: Fragmented systems mean hospitality data never syncs with enterprise CRMs, preventing leadership from understanding event ROI.
